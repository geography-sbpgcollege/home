Professional Profile

Dr. Uma Kant Singh is a dedicated academic with two decades of teaching experience at the undergraduate level and six years at the postgraduate level[cite: 1]. Serving as the Head of the Department of Geography since 2017, he is a leading authority in geospatial technologies and environmental studies at Shri Baldeo P.G. College[cite: 1].

His scholarly footprint includes 29 peer-reviewed research papers, 7 authored books, 3 edited books, and 23 book chapters[cite: 1]. An active voice in the geographical community, he has presented at over 49 international and national conferences and continues to shape academic discourse as a Member of the Board of Studies at Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith University[cite: 1].

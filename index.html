Departmental Overview

Established in 1980, the Department of Geography at Shri Baldeo P.G. College serves as a premier center of excellence. We are dedicated to understanding the earth system, human-environment interactions, and the application of geospatial technologies. By providing rigorous academic training and fostering peer-reviewed research, we shape the analytical minds that map the future of our physical and social landscapes.

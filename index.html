State-of-the-Art Laboratories

Our department houses dedicated research facilities equipped with industry-standard software and hardware to support advanced spatial analysis and physical modeling.

Advanced GIS & RS Lab

Equipped with ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and ERDAS Imagine for high-level spatial data analysis and modeling.

Geomorphology Lab

Housing vast collections of topographical sheets, rock specimens, and physical modeling sandboxes.

On-campus automatic weather station providing live meteorological data for academic research.

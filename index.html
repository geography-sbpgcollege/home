Aims and Scope

The Department of Geography at Shri Baldeo P.G. College is dedicated to advancing the understanding of earth surface processes, human-environment interactions, and geospatial technologies. We offer rigorous academic training for both undergraduate and postgraduate scholars, fostering research that addresses contemporary environmental and regional challenges.
